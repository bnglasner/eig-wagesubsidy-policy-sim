## Finding (paraphrased)
Optimal low-income transfer design hinges on which margin dominates. If responses are
**intensive**, the optimum looks like a classical NIT (high guarantee, high phase-out).
If responses are **extensive**, the optimum looks like the EITC: negative marginal tax
rates at low earnings (a work subsidy) and a small guarantee.

## Why it matters for 80-80
Provides the theoretical lens for the 80-80 design. As a wage-fill conditioned on
working, the 80-80 is in the EITC/work-subsidy family, so its efficiency case rests on
extensive-margin responses dominating — exactly the parameter contested by Kleven (2024).
The sufficient-statistics structure also tells the model which elasticities are
welfare-relevant.
