# Maestas, Mullen & Strand (2013) — Does Disability Insurance Receipt Discourage Work? Using Examiner Assignment to Estimate Causal Effects of SSDI Receipt

- **Tier:** 1 (American Economic Review)
- **Type:** paper
- **DOI:** 10.1257/aer.103.5.1797
- **Source:** https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.103.5.1797
- **Thread:** 3 (target-pool wage-responsiveness: the disabled segment) — entry-margin reality-anchoring pass, 2026-07-09
- **Evidence class:** Measured (ex-post), quasi-experimental. SSA administrative data; random-like assignment of disability examiners with different allowance rates instruments for SSDI receipt.

## Findings (pinned)
- Among the estimated **23 percent of SSDI applicants on the margin of program entry**
  (those whose award depends on which examiner they draw), **employment would have been
  28 percentage points higher had they not received benefits** (measured two years post-
  decision).
- The effect is heterogeneous: **~0 for applicants with more severe impairments, up to
  ~50 percentage points for marginal entrants with the least severe impairments**.
- By construction, the causal work-capacity estimate applies only to the marginal ~23
  percent — inframarginal (clearly qualifying) recipients show no measurable forgone
  employment.

## Why it matters for 80-80 (Q3: how much of the disabled pool is entry-risk?)
The project's 59M non-employed pool includes ~9.7M disabled. This paper is the standard
bound on how much of the disability-benefit population is realistically wage-responsive:
only about **a quarter of the applicant flow is marginal at all**, and even for them
counterfactual employment is ~28pp — not 100pp. Applied to the stock, a defensible
entry-risk share for the disabled segment is small (roughly a quarter of the group times
a ~28-50pp employment response *to complete benefit denial* — a far stronger treatment
than a wage subsidy that stacks against SSDI's earnings test rather than removing the
benefit). Note also the interaction the model must respect: for an SSDI recipient the
80-80 does not add to income freely — SGA earnings limits mean taking subsidized work
risks the entire benefit, so the effective net gain is far below the naive wage fill.
