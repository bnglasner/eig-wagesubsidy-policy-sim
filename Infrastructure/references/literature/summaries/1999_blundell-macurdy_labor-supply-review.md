# Blundell & MaCurdy (1999) — Labor Supply: A Review of Alternative Approaches

## Finding (paraphrased)
The canonical handbook survey of labor-supply modeling. Provides a unifying framework
that lets alternative empirical specifications and their elasticity concepts be
compared (static vs. life-cycle; Marshallian/Hicksian/Frisch; unitary vs. collective
family models). Reviews DiD/natural-experiment approaches, estimation with nonlinear
budget constraints and welfare-program participation, and dynamic extensions
(participation, learning-by-doing, human capital, habit).

## Why it matters for 80-80
Establishes which elasticity concept is appropriate for which counterfactual and how
nonlinear budget sets (kinks, phase-outs) — exactly the structure a wage-fill creates —
should be modeled. The reference frame for choosing the cost model's elasticity inputs.
