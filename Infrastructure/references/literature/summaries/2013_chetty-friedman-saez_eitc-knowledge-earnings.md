# Chetty, Friedman & Saez (2013) — Knowledge Across Neighborhoods and EITC Earnings

- **Tier:** 1 (AER)
- **Type:** paper
- **DOI:** 10.1257/aer.103.7.2683
- **Source:** https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.103.7.2683 ; NBER w18232
- **Thread:** A (program-specific EITC evidence, intensive margin / earnings)
- **Evidence class:** Measured/ex-post, quasi-experimental (bunching + spatial knowledge variation).

## Finding (paraphrased)
Uses cross-area variation in EITC knowledge (proxied by sharp self-employment income
bunching at the refund-maximizing kink) as an instrument. In high-knowledge areas,
wage earners adjust earnings to capture larger refunds; the response is concentrated
on the **intensive margin in the phase-in region**. Demonstrates that earnings
responses to the EITC schedule exist and are knowledge-mediated.

## Why it matters for 80-80
Two lessons for the cost model: (1) behavioral earnings responses to a wage-linked
subsidy can be real but modest and (2) take-up/knowledge frictions mediate the response
— a salience parameter the model may need. The bunching evidence is intensive-margin,
complementing the extensive-margin EITC literature.
