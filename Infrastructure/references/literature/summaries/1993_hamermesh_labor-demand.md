## Finding (paraphrased)
Hamermesh's survey of the labor-demand literature established the long-standing
profession consensus that the **constant-output own-wage elasticity of labor demand is
about −0.3** (commonly summarized as a plausible range of roughly −0.15 to −0.75, with a
central estimate near −0.3), and that the **total (scale + substitution) elasticity is
larger in absolute value than the constant-output elasticity**. This ~−0.3 figure is the
number Lichter, Peichl & Siegloch (2015) revisit and broadly confirm via meta-regression.
