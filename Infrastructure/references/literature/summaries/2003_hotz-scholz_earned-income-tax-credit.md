## Finding (paraphrased)
The standard survey of the EITC as of the early 2000s: conceptual design, participation
and compliance, and effects on labor force participation, hours, marriage, fertility,
and consumption. Synthesizes the consensus that EITC labor-supply effects are positive
for single mothers (extensive margin), small and negative for married mothers, and
near zero for men.

## Why it matters for 80-80
Establishes the demographic heterogeneity of work-conditioned transfer responses
(strong for single mothers, ~0 for men) — a heterogeneity the 80-80 model must carry
because the 80-80 eligible population is far broader than single mothers.
