# Eissa & Liebman (1996) — Labor Supply Response to the EITC

- **Tier:** 1 (QJE)
- **Type:** paper
- **DOI:** 10.2307/2946689
- **Source:** https://academic.oup.com/qje/article-abstract/111/2/605/1938452 ; NBER w5158
- **Thread:** A (program-specific EITC evidence, extensive margin)
- **Evidence class:** Measured/ex-post, quasi-experimental DiD (TRA86; single women with vs. without children).

## Finding (paraphrased)
The 1986 EITC expansion raised the relative labor-force participation of single women
with children by **up to ~2.8 percentage points**, with **no detectable change in hours**
among those already working. Foundational evidence that EITC operates on the extensive
(entry) margin, not the intensive (hours) margin.

## Why it matters for 80-80
The cleanest early demonstration that a work-conditioned transfer moves participation,
not hours — directly relevant since the 80-80 fill is a return-to-work incentive.
The magnitude (a few pp per sizeable expansion) anchors the lower-bound extensive response.
